These terms explain how to use MotorDesk responsibly and what to understand when storing your vehicle records with us.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

1. Using MotorDesk

MotorDesk helps you organise vehicle information, service history, maintenance costs, documents, reminders and reports. By using the service, you agree to use it lawfully and provide information that is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

2. Your account and security

You are responsible for protecting your sign-in credentials and for activity carried out through your account. Tell us if you suspect unauthorised access. Do not share passwords or upload information you do not have permission to store.

3. Your records and privacy

Your vehicle records are intended for your private account. MotorDesk does not sell personal or vehicle data. We do not intentionally access or disclose your private records without your permission, except as reasonably necessary to provide support, operate or secure the service, prevent abuse, or comply with applicable law. Limited hosting, database and storage providers may process data to deliver the service.

Do not treat the “India-first” label as a guarantee that every processing operation or backup is physically located in India unless separately confirmed. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

You remain responsible for the accuracy and legality of information you enter or upload. Generated reports are organisational aids and are not a substitute for original documents, official government records, professional advice or required verification. Keep copies of important originals and verify sale paperwork, including Form 29/30, before use.

6. Acceptable use and availability

Do not attempt unauthorised access, disrupt the service, upload malicious material or violate another person’s rights. Features may change or be temporarily unavailable. Maintain your own copies of critical records.
