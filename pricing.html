Pricing questions

Can I pay for Plus or Family today?

No. These are placeholder plans for planning. Paid-plan checkout and payment collection have not been enabled.

Can I cancel anytime?

There is no paid subscription to cancel yet. Billing terms will be published before paid plans launch.

Who owns my vehicle records?

You provide and manage your vehicle information. Always retain original documents and verify the accuracy of saved records and reports.

What happens if a plan changes?

Downgrade behaviour and storage handling must be defined before paid plans launch. No paid-plan entitlement should be assumed from this page.

Payment design note

Future India payments may use Razorpay for UPI/cards and GST invoices, subject to implementation and compliance review. This is not active today.
