HOW IT WORKS

Four steps to a clearer vehicle history.

Torq Desk works best when you add information as you go. You remain responsible for checking that each record is accurate.

01

Add your vehicle

Enter the vehicle details in your account so records stay grouped correctly.

02

Store documents

Upload supported RC, insurance, PUC and other documents to the relevant vehicle.

03

Log maintenance

Add service dates, odometer readings, work descriptions and costs.

04

Review your report

Use your saved records to prepare a print-friendly report and review upcoming paperwork.
