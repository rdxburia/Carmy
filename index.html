QUICK ANSWERS

A few things worth knowing.

Does Torq Desk replace official vehicle records?

No. It helps organise information you add. Keep your original documents and verify records against the source.

Will Torq Desk send expiry notifications?

Email and push notifications are not available yet. You can review renewal information saved with your vehicle documents.

Can my workshop use Torq Desk today?

The workshop workspace is in early access planning. Join the interest list to hear about onboarding when it opens.
