PRIVACY

Your records deserve care and clarity.

Last updated: 13-10-2026. This summary describes the intended use of Torq Desk and must be reviewed against the live production configuration before launch.

Important

This policy is a draft for product planning, not a claim of end-to-end encryption, a particular data-residency region, or a security certification. Obtain legal review and verify hosting/database settings before relying on it as the final policy.

1. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, information may include account details, vehicle identifiers and specifications, service records, expenses, uploaded vehicle documents, insurance/PUC details and support messages.

2. Why information is used

Information is used to provide account access, save and display vehicle records, manage document uploads, generate reports, support the service, protect against abuse and diagnose technical problems.

3. Infrastructure providers

The service is designed to use Supabase for authentication and database services and Cloudflare services for API delivery and file storage. The actual regions, retention settings, backups and encryption configuration must be checked in the deployed accounts. These providers may process data to deliver the service.

4. Sharing and disclosure

Torq Desk does not sell personal data. Information may be processed by service providers or disclosed where required by law, to protect users and the service, or to respond to a valid legal request. Do not assume a public vehicle-history sharing feature is available until it is explicitly shipped.

5. Security

We use account authentication and access controls, but no internet service can promise absolute security. Do not send passwords or sensitive vehicle documents over ordinary email. Security and retention details depend on the current production configuration.

6. Retention and your choices

Keep your own copies of important documents. In-app data export and complete account deletion are planned capabilities; do not assume they are available until they appear in the app. For assistance, contact hi@torqdesk.in.

7. Children

Torq Desk is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorisation.

8. Updates and contact

This policy may be updated as the service changes. For privacy questions, email hi@torqdesk.in.

Launch requirement: verify the live data flows, storage regions, backup and deletion processes, and obtain legal review before treating this draft as final.
